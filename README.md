## 🧪 Previous Research

### 🦾 Deep Learning for Human Motion Prediction
**Master's Thesis – University of Freiburg / Hahn-Schickard**

Investigated **human joint-angle prediction from wearable IMU sensors** using measured and synthetically generated inertial data.

- Developed **LSTM, CNN-based autoencoder, Attention-LSTM, and DeepConvLSTM** architectures.
- Predicted upper- and lower-body joint kinematics across multiple Activities of Daily Living.
- Generated and evaluated **synthetic IMU data** for data augmentation.
- Evaluated models using participant-independent validation with **MAE and RMSE**.
- Investigated the ability of synthetic sensor data to improve model generalization.

### 🌲 Digital Image Correlation of Pine Cones
**LivMatS Cluster of Excellence – University of Freiburg**

Worked on DFG-funded research investigating the reversible motion of pine cone scales using **Digital Image Correlation (DIC)**.

- Performed high-resolution stereo image acquisition and **3D Digital Image Correlation**.
- Conducted camera calibration and displacement/strain analysis using **VIC-3D and VIC-Snap**.
- Worked with high-precision, sub-pixel measurements of biological structures.
- Collaborated with an interdisciplinary research team across engineering, materials science, and biology.
- Contributed to research resulting in a scientific publication.
